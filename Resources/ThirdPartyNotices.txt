  https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-audio

  https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, MLX build at https://huggingface.co/mlx-community/Kokoro-82M-bf16
Qwen3-TTS 1.7B CustomVoice voice model (Apache-2.0)
  https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice, MLX build at

misaki (Apache-2.0)
  https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki
spaCy and en_core_web_sm (MIT)
  https://github.com/explosion/spaCy
Python (Python Software Foundation License), via python-build-standalone
